### Pitfall regression guards
- **Pitfall 1 (dtype confusion):** `test_fixture_id_is_int64_in_results` and `test_fixture_id_is_int64_in_odds` assert `result.schema["fixture_id"] == pl.Int64` after round-trip.
- **Pitfall 4 (tiny-file partitioning):** `test_results_is_3_level_not_4_level` and `test_odds_is_3_level_not_4_level` assert `not any("matchday=" in str(p) for p in dir.rglob("*"))`.
- **Phase 1 backcompat:** `test_features_still_4_level` asserts `len(features_dir.rglob("matchday=*")) >= 1`.
